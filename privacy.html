Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Who we are

This website is the personal page of Dr. Irakli Gasviani, dental prosthodontist practicing at Dentagram Clinic, 18 Adam Mitskevichi St., Tbilisi, Georgia. You can contact us via WhatsApp, phone or Instagram listed on the contact page.

2. What data we collect

When you use the booking form, we ask for: your name, phone number or messenger username, preferred language, and an optional description of your request. This information is sent directly to the doctor's WhatsApp — this website does not store or transmit your data to any server.

We also use analytics tools (Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel) that collect anonymized usage data such as pages viewed, clicks and scroll depth, via cookies.

3. How we use data

  • To respond to your consultation request via WhatsApp;
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it (analytics only).

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Analytics data is processed by Google and Meta under their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies

Analytics cookies are used by Google Analytics and Meta Pixel. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the website will continue to work.

6. Your rights

You may request deletion of any message you have sent via WhatsApp at any time by contacting the doctor directly.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us via the WhatsApp number listed on the website.
